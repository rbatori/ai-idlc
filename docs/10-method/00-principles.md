# Principles

AI-IDLC is guided by the following principles.

## Intent-driven

Start from the outcome to be achieved, not from a predetermined tool or implementation.

## Integration-first

Prefer composing and configuring existing capabilities before creating new custom software.

## AI-assisted, human-governed

AI may discover, explain, propose, generate and review. Humans remain accountable for material decisions and approvals according to risk.

## Deterministic validation

AI-generated proposals must be checked by deterministic mechanisms wherever practical: schemas, tests, policy checks, simulations, preconditions and postconditions.

## Security-by-design

Trust boundaries, identity, authorization, secrets, least privilege, tenant isolation and failure modes are considered before delivery.

## Evidence-by-design

Intention, decision, approval, execution and result should be traceable.

## Automation-first

Repeatable work should become deterministic automation rather than recurring manual procedure.

## Reversible change

Changes should have rollback, containment or recovery strategies proportional to impact.

## Incremental delivery

Prefer small verified integration increments over large opaque transformations.

## Repository as source of truth

Important intents, decisions, configurations, validation criteria and evidence references should be versioned and reviewable.
